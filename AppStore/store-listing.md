## Description (max 4000)
FinApp is a personal-finance app built around one idea: your money data should
stay on your phone.

Connect your bank through SimpleFin and FinApp pulls in your accounts and
transactions, then organizes them into a clear picture of where your money goes —
without sending your financial data to any server we run. There's no account to
create and nothing to track.

WHAT YOU GET

• Net worth at a glance — every account in one balance, with a history graph that
  grows as you use the app.
• Automatic categories — transactions are sorted into Groceries, Dining,
  Transport, Subscriptions, and more, with rules you can tune.
• Spending insight — see your top categories each month and a six-month trend.
• Recurring bills — FinApp spots subscriptions and repeating charges so nothing
  sneaks up on you.
• Transactions, searchable — filter by category, income, or spending and find any
  charge fast.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Your data is stored only on your device, in an encrypted local database.
• No analytics, no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs.
• The only network connection is to SimpleFin Bridge, to fetch the data you ask
  for.
• Lock the app behind Face ID or your passcode.
• Clear all local data anytime, right from Settings.

JUST LOOKING?

Tap "Preview with sample data" in Settings to explore the whole app with realistic
example data — no bank connection needed.

FinApp requires a SimpleFin account to connect your bank. SimpleFin is a separate
service; see https://simplefin.org.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes" for the reviewer)
FinApp connects to a user's bank through SimpleFin, which requires a user-provided
setup token, so there are no demo bank credentials to share. To review full
functionality without a connection: open the Settings tab and tap "Preview with
sample data" — this loads realistic example accounts, transactions, categories,
and recurring bills so every screen is populated. "Clear all local data" in
Settings removes it again.
