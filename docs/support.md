# FinApp Support

FinApp is a personal-finance app that brings your bank accounts, transactions,
spending categories, recurring bills, and net-worth history together on your
iPhone — with all data stored privately on your device.

## Getting started

1. Open **Settings** in the app.
2. Get a **setup token** from your SimpleFin account (https://simplefin.org).
3. Paste the token and tap **Connect**. Your accounts and transactions sync in.

Want to look around first? Tap **Preview with sample data** on the Settings
screen to explore the app with realistic example data — no connection required.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. There is no server we operate. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**How do I remove my data?**
Settings → **Disconnect** removes the SimpleFin connection. Settings → **Clear
all local data** deletes everything stored on the device. Deleting the app also
removes all data.

**How do I secure the app?**
Settings → **Require Face ID / Passcode**.

**A balance or transaction looks wrong.**
Data comes from your bank via SimpleFin. Tap **Sync Now** in Settings to refresh.
If it still looks off, the source is your bank's data through SimpleFin.
